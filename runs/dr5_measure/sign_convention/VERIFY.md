Scope: only the "sign" track (plus the pilot numbers quoted inside their sign write-up). The "pilot" A_p / A3 / E_n/B_n track was not re-run here.

## Verdict
No material error in the sign conclusion. Recommendation (g1=e1, g2=e2, no flips; PairCounter needs (e1,-e2)) is CONFIRMED.
Two non-blocking problems: (1) their explanation of the 0.17-std "mirror residual" is wrong (it is a TreeCorr rotation dependence, not a mirror/parity effect);
(2) src/scripts text says a single flip "flips the sign of xi-" (it does not; it collapses it to ~0). Plot has independent y-axes.

## Other findings
- `scripts/dr5_convention.py` (note field, line ~76) and its comments state "a single flip flips the sign of xi_-" / "xi_- changes sign with a single flip". Verified false: a single flip drives xi- to ~0 (item 1). The discriminating statistic (r_xim) still works; the wording is wrong. src/kidspipe/measure/xi.py docstring only says "not invariant", which is correct. No src change proposed.
- Production path: `make_treecorr_cat` defaults flip_g1=flip_g2=False and `grep flip` finds no flip in `scripts/dr5_measure_xi_chunked.py`; the pilot used no flips (consistent with the ours/theirs match). Not re-derived: the pilot run itself.
- Plot `plots/dr5_sign_ggl.png` (Read): content matches the table. Layout issues: the top row has independent y-axes per panel (a: +-0.01, b/c: +-0.004..0.006, d: -0.01..0.004), so amplitudes cannot be compared by eye; legends in the upper right of panels a and d sit on top of the 50-100' points/error bars; the suptitle hard-codes "correct convention: gt>0" over all four panels including the b/c/d variants. Labels/units otherwise OK (theta*gamma in arcmin; bottom row sigma units). No labelling error found.
